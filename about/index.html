About The Quiet Argument

The Quiet Argument is an independent space for essays on business, human behaviour, relationships, money, and everyday life. It explores familiar questions from different angles, challenges easy assumptions, and looks for the ideas that deserve a closer look.

What it publishes

Essays and observations in six areas:

  • Business and entrepreneurship
  • Human behaviour and psychology
  • Relationships and friendships
  • Money and financial decisions
  • Personal growth and decision-making
  • Society, culture, and everyday observations

Why it exists

Many familiar questions arrive with ready-made answers. This publication exists to look at them again, from more than one side, and to make a quiet case for the ideas that deserve more attention than they usually get.

Essays are published when they are ready rather than on a fixed schedule. Every essay is listed in the archive.
